A Cohort Analysis model groups customers based on shared characteristics or behaviors over a period of time. 
Typically, cohorts are defined by the time a customer first engaged with your service, 
such as their JOIN_DATE. 

You can then track various behaviors of these cohorts over time (e.g., retention, spend behavior, etc.).

Cohort Analysis Goal:
Group customers into cohorts based on their JOIN_DATE (e.g., the month or quarter they first joined).
Track customer behavior over time, such as their LAST_ACTIVITY_DATE, TOTAL_SPEND, or other metrics.
Calculate cohort retention—how many customers from each cohort are still active in subsequent months.
